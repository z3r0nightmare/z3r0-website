How I Engineer

The common thread across my work is the interaction between physical hardware and analytical engineering.

Engineering approach

Start with the physics. Build the hardware. Let the test tell you what is wrong.

My work spans electromagnetic-thermal FEA, industrial reliability analysis, robotic mechanisms, embedded systems and vibration-based condition monitoring. I prefer projects where CAD is only the beginning — the real work is integrating components, manufacturing the design, measuring behaviour and iterating against evidence.
